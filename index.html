WHAT IS
RAGEROOM

RageRoom is where bad weeks get demolished. It’s a smash room — a sealed, padded space built for exactly one thing: breaking stuff on purpose. We hand you the gear, the tools, and a room packed with crockery, glass, and scrap electronics already written off.

Then we get out of your way.

Solo reset, team offsite, or a birthday you'd rather not sit through — it all works here. Suit up, pick something heavy, and swing until the timer runs out. RageRoom turns a bad week into rubble; you walk out, and the mess stays behind.

Before you swing

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Is it actually safe?
Totally. Every session starts with full coveralls, cut-resistant gloves, and a helmet with a face shield. We run a quick safety brief before you go in, then reset the room after every smash.
What's included in a package?
Every package comes with a fully stocked room — glass bottles, a wooden pallet, and a metal drum barrel — plus a baseball bat for each person and a speaker for your playlist. More people means more bats and more bottles.
What should I wear?
Closed-toe shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dusty. All protective gear is supplied on top of whatever you're wearing.
Can I bring my own stuff to smash?
Go for it — that old monitor, oven, printer, or a chipped dinner set. We'll do a quick safety check on arrival. Bring-your-own electronics run $35–$220 depending on the item.
Is there an age limit?
You must be 18+ to rage solo. Younger ragers can join with a participating adult and a signed waiver.
How do I book and pay?
Pick a package above and fill in the booking form — your slot is confirmed on the spot, with no payment needed up front.
